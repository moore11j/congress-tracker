## Repair and boundaries

The new sec_nonrevenue_financials reader is restricted to the two reviewed symbol/CIK pairs. It selects the latest completed periodic filing, rejects ambiguous or stale identities, and requires each current fact to match that filing's accession and filing date. Each ratio has its own aligned inputs. It retains source hashes, complete calculation evidence, filing provenance, negative values and explicit partial coverage. Revenue, margins and growth are never inferred from missing facts. Other issuers retain their existing revenue gate. Preparation versions change only for these two issuers; normal refresh owns acquisition.

Full-path tests found two earlier acquisition failures: ANIX contains explicitly nil revenue under the XML Schema-instance prefix xs, and BCDA has no current reported revenue. The inline parser now resolves nil by namespace, treats it as unavailable, rejects malformed or conflicting facts, and retains nil evidence. A later nil cannot resurrect an older number through companyfacts merge or continuity annotation; a contradictory number from the same filing remains held. Optional-fact failures retain their existing isolation. Missing revenue is accepted only for the two reviewed CIKs with other current facts, preserving all existing identity, context, currency, date and numeric checks.

This follows the [Inline XBRL specification](https://www.xbrl.org/Specification/inlinexbrl-part1/pr-2013-09-18/inlinexbrl-part1-pr-2013-09-18.html): explicit nil represents a fact without a value. No nil is converted to zero. Preparation, reader and consumer evidence remain distinct from production activation.
