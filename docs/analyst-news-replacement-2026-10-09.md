## Implemented locally

- `finnhub_research.py`: fixed-host, header-authenticated, bounded news/recommendation requests; no redirects, automatic retries, prices or premium endpoints. Response bytes, row count and request duration are bounded. Provider error bodies/keys are not exposed. Missing keys, denials, throttling and malformed responses stay explicit.
- News normalization preserves publisher, original publication time, observation time and provider ID. Canonical URLs remove fragments and known tracking parameters. Duplicate URLs collapse; conflicting IDs are held. Unsafe URLs, conflicting ticker associations, future/old/missing timestamps and missing attribution are rejected. Full article text and images are not republished. Distinct syndicated URLs are not yet reconciled into a common underlying development.
- `replacement_news.py`: separate persistent `InsightsSnapshot` cache keys per Finnhub feed or company, independent of FMP caches. Fifteen-minute freshness, maximum 24-hour stale cache, seven-day article window. General topics share one fetch; forex/crypto use distinct scopes. Macro/treasury/commodity views explicitly use headline keyword filtering. Native comprehensive topic classification is not claimed.
- `NEWS_PROVIDER=finnhub` selects the prepared news path in the existing general, category and ticker getters. Default remains FMP. Public requests, including active ticker panels with force-refresh, only read/enqueue; the existing enrichment worker fetches. Category jobs have distinct dedupe windows. FMP-off does not retire selected replacement news jobs.
- Insights persists actual provider provenance, does not reuse the previous provider's generated commentary, refuses stale input as a new refresh and cannot revive the old FMP snapshot through either reader/fallback. Headline caches older than 24 hours are withheld.
- Legacy watchlist digest news reads prepared replacement caches without fetching or enqueuing and retains delivery preferences and URL deduplication. Selecting replacement news prevents legacy FMP cache materialization into new Events even while FMP remains enabled for other capabilities.
- Recommendations are a **read-only pilot adapter**, not a canonical publication or public analyst-panel switch. Duplicate identical periods collapse; mismatched symbols, malformed counts and conflicting periods are held. Source period and collection time remain distinct; periods older than 45 days are marked stale. No price targets, earnings estimates, score contribution, analyst Events or historical snapshots are manufactured.
- `scripts/validate_finnhub_research.py`: one general-news request plus company-news/recommendation requests for three default symbols, hard maximum five symbols. Sequential spacing and stop-on-auth/denial/throttle, no database, model or email calls. Key comes from `FINNHUB_API_KEY`, never a CLI argument or saved report. Existing evidence files cannot be overwritten.

No schema migration is needed for these prepared caches. This is primary-worktree-only work and has **not** been copied into the release worktree or PR #485.
