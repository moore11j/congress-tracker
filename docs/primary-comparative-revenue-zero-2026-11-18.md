# Primary comparative revenue zero

An untagged dash in a primary income statement can leave the prior comparative revenue period absent from inline XBRL. Missing comparative revenue prevents a trailing-period calculation even when the statement explicitly reports zero.

The added reader admits one narrow, source-qualified layout: a USD US GAAP six-month statement with an explicit June 30 comparative/current heading, one revenue line, research/development and administrative expense lines, and a tagged operating loss. Both year columns must have matching entity-wide contexts and currency, preserve their actual cell geometry, and reconcile revenue less those two expenses exactly to the reported operating loss. The comparative cell must contain an explicit visible dash. Blank, nil, hidden, conflicting, ambiguous and unaligned observations remain unavailable. No missing value is generally converted to zero, and no other expense or revenue components are inferred.

The [Adagene interim statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1818838/000110465926094468/adag-20260812xex99d2.htm) reports the qualifying comparative zero. Its prior operating loss of 15,688,257 equals its two expenses of 12,015,184 and 3,673,073. The current column also reconciles exactly. The [annual statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1818838/000110465926038612/adag-20251231x20f.htm) reports 2025 revenue of 7,670,902. Together with current six-month revenue of 1,619,913, the matched trailing revenue is 9,290,815.

The complete-original fixture restores net and operating margins while preserving current ratio and ROE. Revenue growth, cash-flow and leverage gaps remain. The scoring method is unchanged. The known affected ADAG preparation version rotates; qualification itself uses statement evidence rather than a symbol allowlist. Saved proof retains the source identity, printed period, cell columns, equation inputs and methodology version, and is checked when reused.

Validation includes five complete original sources, adversarial source/proof mutations, reader repeats, existing source qualification tests, preparation persistence and alert consumer tests. The standalone fixture disables external transport and is bounded to 8/10 CPU seconds and 20 wall seconds on Linux. This is partial financial evidence recovery, not full issuer or universe coverage.
