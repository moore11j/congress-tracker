## Replacement and boundaries

Finnhub's free `stock/metric?metric=all` responses captured October 9 include forward/trailing P/E, price/sales, EV/EBITDA, TTM margins/growth and dated ratio series. The [official provider schema](https://github.com/Finnhub-Stock-API/finnhub-go/blob/master/api/openapi.yaml) establishes the endpoint and response envelope, but does not supply per-field definitions or market timestamps. Captured source comparisons establish field units; no magnitude heuristic converts small percentages.

New `free_direct` fundamentals provider preserves every available SEC-calculated value and verified Massive market input, supplementing missing fields with explicitly attributed Finnhub values. It does not acquire prices from Finnhub, infer currency amounts or manufacture net debt/EBITDA. Scalar metrics remain observation snapshots with no invented period or price timestamp. Forward P/E is available without an underlying forecast series or specified forecast horizon; it does not replace annual analyst estimates. Quarterly margin series are not substituted for TTM margins. Current ratio/debt/equity remain plain ratios; dated ROE/ROIC fractions become percentages. Dates beyond150days, future/conflicting periods and wrong securities are rejected/held. Earlier dated ratios cannot fill a newer SEC period.

SEC remains first choice, including its ROE denominator methodology. Finnhub's own ROE may differ; it only fills a missing SEC value. Compound provider identity and a distinct confirmation/outcome methodology preserve historical results and require alert rebaseline on future selection. Leaderboard market inputs remain independently sourced from verified Massive evidence, with no FMP fallback. UI source attribution and optional field provenance distinguish the sources.

Public fundamentals remain FMP. This release enables only free metric preparation inside the existing serialized SEC-ratio job, at most two scopes per run, no more than daily per scope, shared Finnhub budget/cooldown, prioritized active symbols then oldest attempts. No new cron entry, parallel import, price subscription or email. Current cap cannot refresh the whole1751-stock universe daily; active coverage and broader quota/load must be measured before activation. A per-symbol PostgreSQL session lock prevents overlapping fetches while detached from the connection pool; no idle transaction is retained during HTTP. Source failure retains prior prepared evidence and does not relabel it as freshly fetched.
