# Native IFRS annual anchor

A current interim statement may be available while SEC companyfacts omits the preceding annual revenue. The reader can now acquire one directory-qualified 20-F original to fill that gap. This supports annual plus current year-to-date minus prior year-to-date calculations without assuming four comparable quarters.

Qualification requires the same entity, IFRS taxonomy and declared currency; matching total and contract revenue in both interim periods; the latest eligible annual filing; and agreement on every overlapping observation. Assets, current assets, current liabilities and parent equity must overlap at the annual balance date. Existing observations cannot be replaced, and explicit accounting-scope changes retain their holds. The saved proof binds complete original hashes, annual observations, selected inputs and filing metadata.

The complete ADSE fixture includes five SEC originals. It supports EUR trailing revenue of 24,297,000 versus 45,364,000 and six additional ratios. Current ratio is preserved. Negative equity keeps ROE unavailable; leverage and USD cash flow are not inferred. The existing minimum of three scored metrics still leaves the classification and Confirmation Score fundamentals source unavailable.

Preparation versions rotate ADSE's current and historical caches. Other issuers use the same source qualification without a ticker allowlist. An unavailable optional annual source preserves the already qualified current statement.

Tests cover complete acquisition, exact repeat, source and proof mutations, conflicting observations, historical cache invalidation, persisted preparation and existing alert recovery/crossing/rollback behavior. `backend/scripts/verify_native_annual_fixture.py` repeats the complete reader with network disabled and the existing 8/10-second CPU and 20-second wall limits on Linux.
