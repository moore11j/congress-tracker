## Recording and synchronization

Record actual browser search, clicks, transitions and wheel scrolls. Capture the
visible target before clicking; wait for the real result before proceeding.
Curved travel accelerates quickly, eases out and makes a small correction. Pauses
and occasional heading circles replace constant uniform cursor drift. Click rings
are applied only where a click was recorded. Pointer motion is an authored demo,
not a claim of an organically recorded human session.

Each capture stores frame coordinates, click markers, page URLs and semantic
action phrases. The compositor maps those phrases to the continuous ElevenLabs
character alignment. It preserves action order, interpolates the recorded pointer
trace from 12 to 24 fps, and fails on missing or unordered markers. Loading waits
can be shortened in the edit. Captions share the same narration timebase.

The browser uses the authorized administrator's short-lived session. Requests are
read-only and restricted to the demonstrated Walnut routes and data dependencies.
Account identity and suspect numerical fields are obscured in captured pixels;
the live DOM and data are not rewritten. Frame metadata records the obscuration.
