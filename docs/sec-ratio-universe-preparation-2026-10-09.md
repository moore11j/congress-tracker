# Broader free SEC ratio preparation

The small watch and research rotation cannot establish coverage for the retained ranking universe. This opt-in extension adds up to 5,000 successful cached FMP symbols as migration scope, without making FMP requests or publishing a new ranking. It retains the existing isolated SEC ratio caches, shared collection lock, pressure guard, denial stop and lease. Public core fundamentals and market data remain selected independently.

Due watch, research and recently viewed symbols take priority again after 20 hours. Other symbols rotate by oldest attempt, so a larger universe does not starve active users. The default batch remains five; only the universe opt-in permits a configured batch of one to ten. Receipts disclose both scopes, batch size and truncation. There are no canonical, model or email writes.

Fifteen focused Python3.14.2 checks pass across preparation, scheduler and warming, including priority freshness, failed legacy records excluded from scope, repeat acquisition caching, provider refusal and the batch bound. Live release and expanded scheduled coverage remain pending. This is preparation, not evidence that all symbols have adequate financial data or that prices/rankings are ready.
