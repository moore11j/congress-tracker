## Evidence and scope

The SEC's [8-K reading guide](https://www.sec.gov/files/readan8k.pdf) describes Item 2.02 as the reporting route for results of operations. It does not establish that every such filing is an earnings release or transcript. Discovery validates the issuer CIK/ticker and aligned submissions columns, then selects Item 2.02 filings. Filtering precedes the general filing-list cap so high-volume issuers such as JPM retain their earnings filings.

The complete submission must match accession, registrant CIK, form and filing date. Agent-filed accession prefixes are not treated as registrant identifiers. Exhibit filenames must be safe and unique. The primary filing's actual exhibit links/descriptions, release lead and financial content jointly identify a release; financial supplements, CFO commentary, investor summaries and production/delivery materials are excluded. Ambiguous candidates and amendments require review. Fiscal period and transcript coverage are not guessed.
