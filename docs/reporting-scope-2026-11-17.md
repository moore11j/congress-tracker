# Derived financial periods after a discontinued-operation change

NFE's September 2026 report presents the Brazil business as discontinued operations for all periods presented. Its earlier 2025 annual revenue includes that business. Combining the earlier annual amount with the newer interim continuing-operation amounts produces a trailing total on inconsistent reporting scopes.

The source review retains the complete [September report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1749723/000174972326000185/nfe-20260930.htm), [2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1749723/000174972326000032/nfe-20251231.htm) and [separation filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1749723/000174972326000124/nfe-20260911.htm). The separation filing distinguishes historical amounts, the Brazil separation, continuing operations and further transaction adjustments. Its final pro forma column is not reported operating history.

`sec_reporting_scope_v1` annotates the affected revenue and operating concepts for the reviewed CIK. Trailing calculations, derived quarters and growth comparisons require compatible reporting scopes. The review applies only after the completed filing date. Other issuers and earlier observations retain their existing behavior.

Directly reported periods remain available with their original source identity. The current ratio and independently aligned total cash-flow amount remain available. Revenue-dependent trailing ratios and the mixed-scope derived fourth quarter are held. Missing parent-income components remain unavailable and are never assumed zero.

A later accession is not presumed to share the reviewed reporting scope. It can supply directly reported periods and internally consistent derivations, but cannot bridge another accession's scope without further evidence. No amounts are taken from the pro forma adjustment columns.

The complete-source tests cover the source hashes, mixed-scope rejection, reported-period preservation, future accessions, historical availability, cache replacement and alert suppression. The standalone fixture checks exact reader and statement repeats with external transport disabled.
