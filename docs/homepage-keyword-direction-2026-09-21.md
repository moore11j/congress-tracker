Related discovery also returned best stock analysis website, best stock analysis app, free stock analysis websites and stock portfolio analyzer at 100–1,000 each; those phrases have different product expectations.

The broad stock analysis term may mix category, informational and navigational intent. High search volume alone is not a reason to target it as the sole homepage promise. Portfolio-building searches may seek diversification and asset-allocation education, while Walnut's visible product is stronger at finding and researching individual stocks. That intent distinction is an interpretation, not something Keyword Planner itself proves.

## Recommendation

Lead with finding and researching stocks, then explain the distinct benefit: financials, technicals and disclosed activity can be examined together. Keyword demand supports this category direction; it does not prove which headline will convert best.

The live homepage currently says “Build Your Next Winning Portfolio.” That can express an aspiration, but it has weak observed exact-phrase demand and does not immediately explain the product. Avoid making a paid research tool sound like a portfolio manager or promising a winning result.

### Proposed hero

Eyebrow: **STOCK RESEARCH & ANALYSIS**

H1: **Find Your Next Stock. See What's Behind It.**

Subhead: **Explore ranked stocks, compare financials and technicals, and review Congress trades, insider activity and institutional holdings. See where the data agrees—and where it doesn't—before you buy.**

Primary interaction: ticker/company search, placeholder **Search a company or ticker**, button **Analyze a Stock**.

Secondary text link: **Explore Top-Ranked Stocks**.

Access note: **Start with free stock research. Deeper datasets and full rankings require a paid plan.**

Use a real product preview beside/below this copy. Keep the existing guest/Free visibility rules: no public numeric Confirmation Score if it is Premium-gated. “Institutional holdings” describes the product, not a promise that institutional values are free.

Suggested title: **Stock Research & Analysis Platform | Walnut Markets**

Suggested description: **Research stocks with financials, technicals, Congress trades, insider activity and institutional holdings. Explore ranked stocks and start free with Walnut.**

The title, eyebrow, subhead and page content establish the search category. The H1 can communicate the benefit naturally without listing every keyword.

### Landing-page sequence and copy

1. **Find stocks worth a closer look.** Retain the Top 3 preview with dates and real access limits. Link each stock directly to its research page. Explain briefly what the ranking measures; avoid implying guaranteed returns.
2. **See what supports a stock—and what could change your mind.** Show one actual ticker with financials, price/volume and disclosed activity. Present a supporting fact and a real limitation/conflicting fact where available. Never fabricate disagreement just to fill a card. Use source and reporting dates. Link to that ticker.
3. **Research a stock in one place.** Demonstrate the workflow: search a company → open Financials and Ownership → open its Research tab → follow it. Use current product footage/screenshots. Show plan labels where relevant.
4. **Track Congress trades, insider activity and institutional holdings.** Three concise sections linking to the existing canonical landing pages. Explain that disclosures are reported activity, not live buy signals. Do not describe a holder as buying solely because it appears in a holdings list.
5. **Keep following what changes.** Explain saved stocks/watchlists and monitoring using current verified plan capabilities. Offer account creation after the visitor has seen useful research; do not require signup merely to understand the product.
6. **Start free. Choose deeper research when you need it.** Show a concise, verified Free/Premium/Pro comparison and the actual feature benefit that prompts an upgrade. Existing detailed pricing remains one click away.
7. **Common stock-research questions.** Answer what is free, how rankings work, how disclosure timing works and where a visitor should start. Link to fuller methodology. Retain relevant existing sections when useful, but give the above path priority instead of equal prominence to every tool.

## Match different searches to different destinations

| Search intent | Preferred destination | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Stock research platform | Homepage | Broad product proposition and clear first action |
| Stock analysis tools | Existing /stock-analysis-tools | Explain tools with real examples and access levels |
| Stock screener / best stock screener | Existing screener and suitable indexable explanatory page | Demonstrate screening; verify rendered canonical before SEO rollout |
| Stock comparison tool | A substantive indexable comparison landing page with working examples | Generic app /compare currently redirects to noindex /compare/_/_; do not send organic targeting there |
| Finviz alternative / better than Finviz | Existing /compare/walnut-markets-vs-finviz | Specific, evidenced comparison; no blanket superiority claim |
| How to research stocks / how to pick stocks | One strong educational walkthrough linked to product | Show the job being done with a real stock; validate content freshness |
| How to build a stock portfolio | Educational guide only if genuinely covering diversification and allocation | Not the main product promise based on current capabilities |
| NVIDIA ownership, NBIS vs CRWV | Existing ticker/research pages | Answer the specific question and link to the next relevant action |

Do not create near-duplicate landing pages for every synonym. Walnut already has stock-research-software, stock-analysis-platform and stock-analysis-tools pages; compare their impressions/query overlap and content before deciding whether consolidation is warranted. This review does not establish actual keyword cannibalization.

## Finviz capture strategy

Use the existing indexed comparison page. Proposed heading: **Looking for a Finviz Alternative? Explore Walnut's Stock Research.** Explain the actual differences in screening, ticker research, disclosures, briefs, pricing and access, with current verification. A useful supporting line is **Found a stock in Finviz? Take a closer look in Walnut.** This captures the after-screening workflow without asserting Walnut has every Finviz feature or is categorically better.

Keep Finviz out of the homepage H1. Its alternative-search demand is a small, relevant subset; the homepage must also make sense to visitors who have never used Finviz. “Finviz but more” creates a broad feature promise the data here does not substantiate.

## Measurement

Before evaluating the proposal, verify signup/payment tracking from the earlier approved work. Compare organic landing sessions, ticker searches/opens, signup completion and paid conversion by landing page and acquisition source. In GSC, compare query/page impressions and clicks for the selected topic clusters over equivalent windows. At low traffic, a short test will not establish a statistically reliable conversion lift. Hold a coherent version long enough to observe evidence instead of changing the hero weekly.
